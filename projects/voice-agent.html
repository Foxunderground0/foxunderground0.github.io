Streaming Voice Agent

A streaming speech interface with online transcription, response generation, and audio pipeline control.

2024 to 2025Professional projectSoftware

WebSockets · WebRTC VAD · Whisper · GPT 4

Motivation

A useful spoken agent needs to respond quickly despite capture, endpointing, transcription, generation, and playback delays.

Implementation and scope

  • Built the streaming service in Node.js with WebSockets.
  • Integrated WebRTC voice activity detection with Whisper and GPT 4.
  • Optimized buffering and stage overlap across the audio pipeline.

Outputs

  • Reduced end to end response latency to under four seconds.
