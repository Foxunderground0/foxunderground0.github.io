About

I am a Computer Science undergraduate at Lahore University of Management Sciences with a minor in Computer Engineering. My research interests are embedded systems, hardware security, firmware security, computer architecture, and operating systems.

I worked as a research assistant at SYSNET Lab with Dr. Naveed Anwar Bhatti and at CHASE Lab with Dr. Muhammad Ali Siddiqi. My work includes compromised flash programmers, runtime hardware Trojan detection, transiently powered sensing, embedded AI, and low power sensing platforms.

Publications

Accepted publications

  1. Motion-Coupled Sensing. When the State Change Powers Its Own Sensing.

    IEEE MASS 2026 · Co-author

    arXiv PDF Project notes

    • Co-developed motion coupled sensing to eliminate battery maintenance and idle polling. Hinge motion powered state measurement and wireless reporting.
    • Designed ATmega328P hardware and LoRa firmware. Cut a firmware loop from 520 to 7 CPU cycles using fixed point arithmetic.
    • Built a stepper driven rig and verified 10,000 transmissions without visible wear. Field reliability reached 99.3 percent across 5,945 lid actuations at five campus sites.

  2. Kissan-Dost. Bridging the Last Mile in Smallholder Precision Agriculture with Conversational IoT.

    IEEE DCOSS-IoT 2026 · Co-author

    arXiv PDF Project notes

    • Co-developed Urdu WhatsApp text and voice advice so smallholder farmers could act on live sensor readings without interpreting dashboards.
    • Built a multi hop ESP-NOW monitoring mesh and ESP32 gateway for soil and climate sensing.
    • Deployed at two field sites for 45 days each. Farmers maintained near daily use and used the guidance to inform irrigation.

Submitted manuscript

  1. PARDA. On Device Audio Privacy for Smart Glasses Using Small Language Models.

    IEEE PerCom 2027 · First author

    Manuscript Project notes

    • Designed context aware redaction and voice replacement for smart glasses audio. Protected bystander identity and private attributes inferred across conversation turns.
    • Labeled 717 CANDOR conversations. Combined speaker profiles with retrieval and a distilled 2B model trained through preference optimization. Deployed Q4 inference and ONNX audio on Raspberry Pi 5.
    • Reduced mean privacy leakage from 0.502 to 0.378 on 143 held out conversations with 0.900 utility. The audio front end achieved a 0.77 weighted real time factor.

Manuscripts in preparation

  1. Programmer in the Middle. Firmware Implantation via Flash Programmers.

    Joint first author

    Manuscript Project notes

    • Demonstrated a firmware supply chain trust gap. Compromised programmers implanted code after review while leaving host binaries and verification results unchanged.
    • Modified USBasp firmware for AVR reset vector hijacking and stub injection. Spoofed host readback checks.
    • Reverse engineered ST-Link V2 firmware and tested custom programmer firmware across three target boards.

  2. On Chip Behavioral Monitoring for Runtime Hardware Trojan Detection.

    Joint first author

    Manuscript Project notes

    • Designed transaction level Trojan monitoring to catch rare trigger attacks that can pass predeployment tests.
    • Built a trace collection pipeline for expected and Trojan affected execution across computational and application workloads. Developed a 1D CNN classifier.
    • Specified an FPGA monitor architecture. Planned generalization studies across processor and cache configurations with latency and resource evaluation.

Preprints

  1. LLM-Enhanced Wearables for Comprehensible Health Guidance in LMICs.

    arXiv 2026 · Co-author

    arXiv PDF Project notes

    • Co-developed plain language WhatsApp health feedback to make low cost wearable data understandable to users with limited health literacy.
    • Designed a $12.72 screenless ATmega328P band and BLE firmware. Combined PPG with temperature and motion sensing.
    • Enabled a 20 participant 96 hour study. Mean self rated health data comprehension rose from 3.05 to 3.60 out of 5.

  2. WatchTower. Transformer Based Open Set Network Anomaly Detection.

    Preprint · Joint first author

    Project notes

    • Developed open set intrusion detection for edge devices that must identify attacks absent from their training data.
    • Combined a transformer with 28 packet level features and PROSER. Optimized INT8 inference with TVM on Raspberry Pi 5.
    • Reached 3.82 ms inference latency and 93.66 percent classification accuracy on the deployed model.
