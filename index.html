About

I am a Computer Science undergraduate at Lahore University of Management Sciences with a minor in Computer Engineering. My research interests are embedded systems, hardware security, firmware security, computer architecture, and operating systems.

I worked as a research assistant at SYSNET Lab with Dr. Naveed Anwar Bhatti and at CHASE Lab with Dr. Muhammad Ali Siddiqi. My work includes compromised flash programmers, runtime hardware Trojan detection, transiently powered sensing, embedded AI, and low power sensing platforms.
