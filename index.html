About

I am a Computer Science undergraduate at Lahore University of Management Sciences with a minor in Computer Engineering. My research focuses on the security and operation of embedded systems.

I worked at SYSNET Lab with Dr. Naveed Anwar Bhatti and CHASE Lab with Dr. Muhammad Ali Siddiqi. I developed sensing hardware and experimental infrastructure. My current research examines compromised flash programmers and runtime hardware Trojan detection.

Publications

Accepted publications

  1. Motion-Coupled Sensing. When the State Change Powers Its Own Sensing.

    IEEE MASS 2026 · Co-author

    arXiv PDF Hardware and firmware Project notes

    • Co-developed batteryless hinge sensing and LoRa reporting. Hinge motion powers each measurement and transmission without periodic polling or battery replacement.
    • Built ATmega328P hardware and a stepper-driven rig for 10,000 actuations. Cut a firmware loop from 520 to 7 cycles. Field reliability reached 99.3 percent over 5,945 actuations at five sites.

  2. Kissan-Dost. Bridging the Last Mile in Smallholder Precision Agriculture with Conversational IoT.

    IEEE DCOSS-IoT 2026 · Co-author

    arXiv PDF Project notes

    • Built a multi-hop ESP-NOW mesh and ESP32 gateway for soil and climate data. Delivered Urdu WhatsApp text and voice guidance without requiring farmers to interpret dashboards.
    • Across two 45-day deployments, a five-participant pilot found near-daily chatbot use and irrigation decisions informed by its advice.

Submitted manuscript

  1. PARDA. On Device Audio Privacy for Smart Glasses Using Small Language Models.

    IEEE PerCom 2027 · First author

    Manuscript Project notes

    • Designed on-device audio privacy using a retrieval-aware 2B model. Redacted private attributes inferred across conversation turns and replaced speaker voices.
    • Across 143 held-out conversations, model-judged privacy leakage fell from 0.502 to 0.378 while utility rose from 0.875 to 0.900. Evaluated quantized deployment on Raspberry Pi 5.

Manuscripts in preparation

  1. Programmer in the Middle. Firmware Implantation via Flash Programmers.

    Joint first author

    Pre-Release Project notes

    • Demonstrated firmware implantation through compromised flash programmers. Modified USBasp to inject AVR code while returning clean host readback checks.
    • Reverse engineered ST-Link V2 and tested custom programmer firmware on three target boards. Exposed a trust gap between reviewed binaries and programmed hardware.

  2. On Chip Behavioral Monitoring for Runtime Hardware Trojan Detection.

    Joint first author

    Pre-Release Project notes

    • Built a transaction-trace pipeline and 1D CNN classifier for runtime hardware Trojan monitoring. Targets malicious execution that can escape pre-deployment tests.
    • Specified an FPGA monitor architecture. Evaluation across workloads, processor configurations and caches remains ongoing.

Preprints

  1. LLM-Enhanced Wearables for Comprehensible Health Guidance in LMICs.

    arXiv 2026 · Co-author

    arXiv PDF Project notes

    • Designed a $12.72 screenless wearable and BLE firmware for plain-language WhatsApp health feedback. Addresses affordability and difficulty interpreting sensor data.
    • Supported a 20-participant 96-hour study. Mean self-rated health-data comprehension rose from 3.05 to 3.60 out of 5.

  2. WatchTower. Transformer Based Open Set Network Anomaly Detection.

    Preprint · Joint first author

    Project notes

    • Developed open-set intrusion detection for edge devices. Combined a transformer and 28 packet-level features to identify traffic outside known attack classes.
    • Optimized INT8 deployment on Raspberry Pi 5. Reported 3.82 ms inference and 93.66 percent classification accuracy.
